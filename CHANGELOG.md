## 0.0.1

Initial release of `flutter_toggle_switch`.

### Widgets

- `FlutterToggle` — unified toggle with pill, underline, stacked cards, and magnetic blob styles
- `PulseToggleSwitch` — binary switch with pulsing ring
- `DayNightToggleSwitch` — animated sun/moon theme switch
- `PowerToggleSwitch` — power button with rotating glow ring
- `SegmentBarToggleSwitch` — sequential LED segment indicator

### Styles

- 7 decoration styles: `solid`, `glass`, `neumorphic`, `outlined`, `tonal`, `floating`, `aurora`
- 5 animation styles: `slide`, `fade`, `scale`, `elastic`, `morph`
- 5 pill styles: `filled`, `glass`, `outline`, `soft`, `liquid`

### Features

- Haptic feedback, shimmer, icon wiggle, ripple effects
- Async cancel guard (`cancelToggle`)
- Semantic labels and keyboard navigation
- Horizontal and vertical layouts with per-item custom sizing
